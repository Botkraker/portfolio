IoT · Computer Vision

Fridguard, an AI-Powered Smart Refrigerator

Co-developed an AI-powered fridge-monitoring system to reduce household food waste, combining an AI camera, environmental sensors, and a mobile app.

Built an edge-to-cloud pipeline. A Raspberry Pi 4 with a camera and temperature, air-quality, and door sensors captures data every 5 seconds and posts it to a centralized Node.js hub, which orchestrates a YOLO-based computer-vision service for food-item recognition and a MongoDB store for inventory tracking.

Event-driven Firebase alerts flag spoilage-risk conditions, with REST APIs powering an Android app for real-time monitoring.

  • IoT
  • YOLO
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Node.js
  • MongoDB
  • Android
  • Firebase
