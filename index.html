IoT · Computer Vision

Fridguard — AI-Powered Smart Refrigerator

Co-developed an AI-powered fridge-monitoring system to reduce household food waste, combining an AI camera, environmental sensors, and a mobile app. Built an edge-to-cloud pipeline: a Raspberry Pi 4 with camera and temperature/air-quality/door sensors captures data every 5 seconds and posts it to a centralized Node.js hub, which orchestrates a YOLO-based computer-vision service for food-item recognition and a MongoDB store for inventory tracking. Event-driven Firebase alerts flag spoilage-risk conditions, with REST APIs powering an Android app for real-time monitoring.

Parkinson's Disease Detection from Voice Biomarkers

Non-invasive detection pipeline over 195 voice recordings (22 acoustic features) from 31 individuals. Reduced dimensionality from 22 to 6 components with PCA, corrected class imbalance with oversampling, and benchmarked 5 classifiers with grid-search tuning.

Best model (MLP): 90% accuracy, 97% recall on positive cases.
